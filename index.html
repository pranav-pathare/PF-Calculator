PF wage = lower of basic salary and the selected ceiling. Monthly CTC = gross salary + employer contribution (EPS + EPF). Your CTC is held fixed at the gross you enter plus the employer share at the ₹15,000 ceiling; at a higher ceiling the extra employer share is taken out of gross, which lowers net take-home. Net take-home = gross − employee PF − professional tax. Employer's 12% is split into EPS (8.33%) and EPF; EPS is always calculated on wages up to ₹15,000, whichever ceiling is selected. Figures are indicative; check your payslip or employer for exact treatment.
